    How I got here

    I started as the person writing requirements. I ended up building the systems too.

    In my last semester at Imam University I joined Monshaat as a co-op business analyst. I sat with HR and Finance, wrote user stories, and worked in two-week sprints with the developers.

    I kept noticing problems that AI could fix, so I learned to build the fixes myself. After I graduated, I stayed on as a contract business analyst on their AI projects.

    I still open every project by asking who's stuck, and what fixed would look like to them. The building comes after that.
